How to Register

Registration Rules

group_add

1. Form a Team

One person must click "Create Team". This person automatically becomes the Leader of the squad.

search

2. Invite Members

Tell your friends your exact Team Name. They need to search for it in the lobby and click "Join".

lock

3. Submit & Lock

Once you have exactly 5 members, the Leader must click "Submit & Lock Team". Your spot is NOT secured until the team is locked!

warning Important Rules

  • Only the first 10 Locked Teams will be allowed to play.
  • You cannot be a member of more than one team at a time.
  • Once a team is locked, nobody can leave and the Leader cannot kick anyone.
